handstorm soundboard

1. speech to text begins

palm listener opens the mic. rising = "I'm listening".

2. llm results available

new items land in the finger slots.

3. pulling text out

one tick per step of movement. drag a slider (or use arrow keys); pitch-stepping variants climb with distance.

4. pulled out

strip passes the speed threshold and detaches from the finger.

5. drop content on canvas

pinch released, text is placed.

6. link content to finger

fingertip dwells on placed text, line attaches.

7. shake off

hand roll shake drops titles or snaps links.
